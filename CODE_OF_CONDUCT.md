# <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/users.svg" alt="" width="20" height="20"> Code of Conduct

## <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/heart-handshake.svg" alt="" width="20" height="20"> Our commitment

The TuneX community welcomes contributors, maintainers, testers, and users with different experience levels and backgrounds. We want technical discussion to remain constructive, inclusive, and focused on improving the software.

We commit to fostering a harassment-free environment regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/check-circle.svg" alt="" width="20" height="20"> Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and professional.
- Critique code, experiments, and ideas rather than attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and explain trade-offs where possible.
- Welcome questions and assume good faith while remaining open to correction.
- Credit others for their contributions and respect licensing.
- Respect maintainers' time, decisions, and requests to de-escalate.
- Handle vulnerability reports and personal information responsibly.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Evidence-based debate about algorithms, Elo testing, chess rules, performance, or implementation is part of a healthy engineering community.

## <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/triangle-alert.svg" alt="" width="20" height="20"> Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or threats.
- Hate speech, discriminatory remarks, or demeaning comments.
- Personal insults, sustained trolling, or deliberately disruptive arguments.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or sexualized content.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Repeated unwanted contact after someone has asked you to stop.
- Knowingly sharing malicious code or deceptive benchmark results as genuine evidence.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/compass.svg" alt="" width="20" height="20"> Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to TuneX's GitHub issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, documentation, and other project-managed communication spaces. It also applies to public interactions where someone is explicitly representing TuneX or its community.

## <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/message-square-warning.svg" alt="" width="20" height="20"> Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, **contact the repository maintainers privately** through an available GitHub contact method. Include relevant links, dates, and a description of what happened, if you feel comfortable doing so.

Do **not** post sensitive personal information or a confidential conduct report in a public GitHub issue. If there is no private contact method available, you may use GitHub's platform abuse-reporting tools for violations of its policies.

Maintainers should limit disclosure to people who need the information to investigate and respond. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

## <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@latest/icons/shield-check.svg" alt="" width="20" height="20"> Enforcement

Project maintainers may take action that is proportionate to the conduct and its impact. Actions can include:

1. A private reminder or request to change behavior.
2. A formal warning or removal of inappropriate content.
3. Temporary limits on participation.
4. Permanent exclusion from project-managed spaces for serious or repeated violations.

When reasonable, maintainers will consider context, pattern, severity, and the safety of affected people. Retaliation for good-faith reporting is prohibited.
